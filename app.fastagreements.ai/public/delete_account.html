Account Deletion

Learn how to permanently delete your Fast Agreements account and associated data.

1. Delete via Mobile App

  • Log in to your account on the Fast Agreements mobile application.
  • Go to your Profile.
  • Select Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompts.

2. Contact Support

  • Send an email to our support team requesting account deletion.
  • Please contact us at fastagreements@gmail.com.
  • We will process your request and delete your account and associated data.
